Z-FUEL is a commercially marketed reverse-energy beverage developed by Zeta Site. Introduced in 1989, the product was designed as an alternative to conventional energy drinks, with a focus on promoting relaxation and reducing physical activity. Z-FUEL utilizes Zeta Site's proprietary De-Energizing Compound, which the company developed to produce effects opposite to those associated with traditional energy-boosting beverages. The product is marketed as a reverse-energy drink.

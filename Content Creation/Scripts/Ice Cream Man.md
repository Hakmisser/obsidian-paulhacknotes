Right now we live in a time where there is no shortage of good horror movies. Just this past year we got movies like Iron Lung, Backrooms, Obsession, Resident Evil, and more which are all phenomenal in their own ways, but through all the blood and the scares there seems to be one movie that flew right under the radar. However, with all of these horror movies coming out I think I've had my fill, but I always leave room for dessert.

"Ice Cream Man" directed and written by Eli Roth, is a horror comedy released in theaters August 7th, 2026, and let me tell you the ratings and reviews for this movie were not kind at all and for good reason. This movie is filled with a ridiculous amount of gore and children offing people, it's basically just children unaliving people for an hour and twenty-six minutes, but the YouTube movie comments are great!
